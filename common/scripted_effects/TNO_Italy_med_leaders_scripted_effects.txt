### Italy - Mediterranean rework leaders.
###
### TNORA does not use the common/characters system, so the rework's leaders
### are declared the way the rest of the mod declares them: create_country_leader,
### called at the point the old content called promote_character. One effect per
### person; a person with more than one ideology gets one block per ideology, the
### way the character file gave him more than one country_leader entry.
###
### Names, portraits, ideologies and description keys are carried over verbatim
### from common/characters/Triumvirate - Italy PW.txt. The leader traits that file
### listed (ITA_the_black_augustus, the_new_lenin, ITA_the_aviator and the rest)
### are defined in neither branch, so they are not carried over - a traits block
### naming an undefined trait throws on every call.

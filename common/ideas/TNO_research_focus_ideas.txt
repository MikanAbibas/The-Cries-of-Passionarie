### TNO Research Priorities
### Law category swapped from the button under the research slots (see common/scripted_guis/TNO_ResearchFocus.txt)
###
### Every priority lists the same 15 categories: the focused field at +0.10, every
### other field at -0.05. No global research_speed_factor is used.
###
### Those 15 categories were picked so that no technology in common/technologies
### carries two of them - every tech matches exactly one line, so nothing stacks.
### The sub-categories (fighter, MBT, dd_tech, radar_tech, cat_maneuver_warfare, ...)
### are deliberately left out; they always sit on a tech that already matches one of
### the 15, and listing them would double or triple the bonus on that tech.
###
### Categories:
###   Doctrine    - land_doctrine, air_doctrine, naval_doctrine
###   Land/Naval  - infantry_weapons, special_forces_tech, armor, artillery,
###                 naval_equipment, support_tech
###   Air         - air_equipment, helicopter, missile
###   Industrial  - industry, electronics, jet_technology
###
### National academias go in this same slot. They are declared here like any other
### priority, weight the 15 categories however they like, and are handed out at
### runtime with TNO_unlock_research_focus - see the checklist at the bottom of
### common/scripted_effects/TNO_research_focus_scripted_effects.txt.
